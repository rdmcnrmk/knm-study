# KNM curriculum audit and study map

Reviewed 14 November 2026 against the [official KNM attainment targets effective 1 July 2025](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2024-15802.html).

All 78 numbered knowledge targets have at least one linked study section. This is a section-level editorial mapping. It is not a certification that every sub-indicator or example is exhaustively covered, nor an official DUO endorsement. Read all linked sections for a target; a section may address only one part. Individual regional knowledge is practised with the local-notes exercise. Changing political names and personal eligibility must be rechecked.

## What was expanded

32 new bilingual lessons, 32 explained independent questions, 32 vocabulary entries, a province position guide and the 78-item self-assessment checklist. Course totals: 74 lessons, 82 independent questions, 80 words. Prior completion IDs are retained.

## Boundaries and important distinctions

- All site questions are independent practice. Official DUO practice remains on the linked DUO website. Mock question count, equal topic weighting and accuracy percentage are study choices, not official pass rules.
- New lesson sources supplement the supplied PDF; the original text companion remains unchanged. Legal and administrative summaries are for KNM study and do not replace checking rules for an individual situation.
- The province graphic is schematic, not a boundary map; the official CBS map is linked. Regional examples must be completed for the learner’s own town.
- Interpreter arrangements/payment, children’s dental/physiotherapy cover, benefit entitlement, non-citizen voting, dual nationality and parental liability have conditions or exceptions. Lessons state this rather than presenting blanket rules.
- “Opstalverzekering required” can refer to a lender’s mortgage condition; it is not a blanket statutory duty for all homeowners.
- GP consultation is exempt from the mandatory deductible, but subsequent tests or medicines can be subject to it.
- The prime minister and King entry is dated 14 November 2026; it is not live data.
- Self-assessment checkmarks express the learner’s confidence. They do not demonstrate exam readiness on their own.
